# Before public launch

- Confirm the legal operator, contact details, legal notice and privacy text.
- Confirm permission to publish supplied property photos.
- Preserve regional image authors, source links, licences and transformation notices.
- Confirm guest capacity, bed arrangements, booking terms and outdoor-use boundaries.
- Describe the guest unit and separate host loft accurately.
- Confirm the final contact destination; the current planner only prepares text for Airbnb.
- Recheck destination links, closures and seasonal information with operators.
- Keep the no-prices requirement.
- Describe press as coverage of the village and former school, not an accommodation endorsement.
- After content approval, review preview labels, robots.txt, noindex meta and X-Robots-Tag together before making the site public.
- Configure the final domain and update canonical, hreflang and social-image URLs in the generator.

Repository preparation does not make the site public or change its domain.
