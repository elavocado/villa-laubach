# Brief for the website build

This is the implementation handoff from the research, not a published site. Latest factual authority: FINAL-RESEARCH-AR.md. Date: 29 October 2026.

## Product and audience

German-first, equivalent English version. A historic self-contained holiday accommodation in Laubach, Eifel, with regional discovery as the second main reason to visit. Main audiences: families and friends, walkers, cultural visitors and Nürburgring visitors. Do not present the property as an event venue, spa, retreat business or officially classified luxury hotel without further evidence.

## Required visitor journey

1. Real house image, name, location and clear enquiry button.
2. Short introduction to the accommodation, with its boundaries and privacy accurately described.
3. Former-school story, with building dates attributed to the official heritage register.
4. Rooms and amenities, using only owner-confirmed details and correctly mapped photographs.
5. Six selected excursions leading to a broader regional guide.
6. Arrival information and optional directions link; no invented journey times.
7. FAQ, enquiry form and actual operator/contact information.

Use no prices, nightly rates, discounts or price comparison claims. Primary CTA: Aufenthalt anfragen / Enquire about your stay. State clearly that an enquiry does not confirm a booking. A secondary external booking-platform link is optional. Do not activate a form without a real recipient and verified delivery.

## Publication decisions

Design can proceed now. Publication requires legal operator/contact details, image rights, accurate guest-unit boundaries, bathroom/bed layout and current house rules. The exact conversion date and an archival photograph are optional story enhancements; omit them if not established.

The guest unit and host loft are separate units in the same building according to the indexed host description. Avoid entire-building exclusivity claims. Confirm whether garden, terrace, entrance and parking are private or shared. Keep conflicting departure-time and age-policy values out of public copy until the owner provides one current policy.

## Editorial and accessibility checks

Correct location: 56759 Laubach in the Eifel, Rhineland-Palatinate. No Hessen or Hunsrück facts about a different Laubach. Geierlay is a wider excursion in the Hunsrück; label it accordingly. Destination photographs must never imply a view from the house. Preserve author/licence information for each image derivative. Use readable typography, strong contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and descriptive alternative text in both languages.

No fake testimonials, invented stars, simulated availability, placeholder contact details or stock photographs presented as the actual property. Recheck operational information at launch, especially trail/bridge works, seasonal attractions and transport.
